FONTLOG for Ezenwa Grotesk

Ezenwa Grotesk is a modified version of Archivo, released under the SIL Open
Font License 1.1 (see OFL.txt). Archivo declares no Reserved Font Name, and
the modified font does not use the Archivo name.

Base: Archivo variable fonts (roman and italic, wght 100-900, wdth 62-125)
from https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/archivo

Changes, all made by fit.py:
- Light, Regular, Medium, Semibold and Bold, each with an italic. Lowercase,
  capitals and figures each come from the axis location whose stem weight
  and mean width match the targets in SPEC.md.
- Cap height 640 and ascender 640 in a 1000-unit em; x-height 470 at Regular
  and descender -170.
- Curves made slightly rounder throughout.
- f hook and t foot rebuilt as arcs cut from the o, with their counters kept
  open at heavy weights, and the t's crossbar lengthened to meet its foot;
  their accented forms follow, and the f ligatures are no longer substituted.
- Widths adjusted per letter class, by at most 3%; spacing adjusted per
  class, with f and t spaced to match n, and kerning added for any common
  pair that touched.
- Figure variants share the figures' weight and width; math signs share the
  tabular width.
- Line metrics, style table, version and Windows bounds set so fontbakery
  reports no failures.

No outlines, kerning or per-glyph widths were copied from any other font.
Only aggregate measurements were used as targets: heights, stem weight, and
class mean widths and spacing.
